Autumn

Nobody wants to be the rejector
The audience seated becomes the projector
I clawed at your legs til you were my protector
I cried through the night when you gave me that lecture
Now I'm so depressed that I want to live
And fumbling feelings are all I can give
And twice now a touch tried to temper my woes
And like twine it wove my novemberized clothes
So we stood on the curb and the month grew sick
Loose lovers in loafers without rocks to kick
